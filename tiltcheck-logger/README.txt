TiltCheck Logger (alpha)
1. Unzip this folder.
2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and pick the unzipped folder.
4. Pin it. Play like normal on bink.bet or nuts.gg.
5. Updating from 0.1: replace the folder contents, then hit the reload arrow on the extension card. Your logs stay.
It counts time only while the casino tab is visible and you've touched mouse or keys in the last 3 minutes.
A session ends after 10 minutes away. Click Export CSV anytime to get the raw data.
